How it works

From a map pin to a phone call in three taps.

Open the map

Every room and shared apartment on one map, with the monthly rent right on the pin. No account needed to look.

Open the listing

Real photos of the room and the apartment, the rent, the room size, how many roommates, and the lister's real phone number.

Call

One tap opens your phone app and dials. There's no in-app chat, no lead form, and nobody taking a cut.

Who you'd live with

A room is easy to judge from photos. The people aren't.

Anyone can show you four pictures of a bedroom. Roomie shows you the people. Fill in a short profile — five sliders on how you actually live — and Roomie scores how closely you match everyone else looking to share a place, best matches first.

  • Five lifestyle spectra: quiet or social, tidy or relaxed, early bird or night owl, guests rarely or often, own thing or share everything
  • A match score on every profile, worked out against yours
  • Endorsements from former and current roommates, friends and landlords
  • Lifestyle tags on listings too — quiet, academic, pet friendly, kosher, LGBTQ+ friendly and more

Match scores and profiles need a free account on both sides — that's the one thing Roomie asks you to sign up for. The map, the listings and the phone numbers stay open to everyone.

Safety & privacy

You stay in control.

Report or block anyone

Every listing and every profile can be reported. Any user can be blocked, and blocked people drop straight out of your Roommates list.

Delete your account from the app

One button on your profile, confirmed with your password. No email to send, no form to wait on.

Your location never leaves your phone

GPS is used on your device only, to centre the map. It is never uploaded and never collected.

Contact happens by phone call — Roomie has no in-app chat. Phone numbers appear publicly on the listing they belong to, the way they would on a printed flyer. The privacy policy spells all of it out.
